# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primarily the developer building this as a portfolio piece, testing it themselves. Secondary audience is recruiters/reviewers casually clicking through a live demo on a laptop, in good lighting, exploratory mood, not under time pressure. Not (yet) a daily-driver tool for real job seekers under stress.

## Product Purpose

An AI resume-to-job-description matcher: upload a resume once, run it against job descriptions, get a match score with a skills breakdown and improvement suggestions, saved to a dashboard of past analyses. Success looks like a reviewer opening the dashboard and immediately reading it as a serious, well-engineered tool, not a tutorial project.

## Brand Personality

Precise, confident, technical. Reference lane: Linear / Raycast — dark, sharp, minimal-but-confident. Restrained neutral palette with one precise accent color, tight spacing, fast/snappy motion. Feels engineered, not decorated.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS-template look: pastel gradient hero blobs, bouncy/playful illustration style, card-grid-of-icons homepages, glassmorphism as decoration, gradient text. Nothing that reads as a tutorial/bootcamp project.

## Design Principles

- Competence-first: the UI should read as engineered, not decorated. Precision over flourish.
- Restrained palette, one deliberate accent: color earns its use, it doesn't fill space.
- Motion confirms, it doesn't perform: fast, purposeful transitions (state changes, data arriving), never idle/ambient animation.
- Data is the content: scores, skills, and suggestions are the interface. Chrome (cards, borders, icons) stays quiet around them.
- Dark-first: matches the Linear/Raycast reference lane and the "glancing at your own tool" usage context.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA contrast targets. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (disable/shorten transitions). No other special accommodations noted; solo/low-stakes usage context.
